The ticket has a very small, warm shadow: a crisp contact edge and a short, soft fall below. It is a box-shadow on the ticket itself, so it tilts with the card. While the ticket tilts, the shadow reaches toward the lifted edge, getting longer, softer and slightly deeper there and tighter where the ticket presses down. For example, a cursor at the top-left lifts the bottom-right, so the shadow grows at the bottom-right (user request). It glides back to the resting shadow on the same spring. Do not bring back the large dark glow or a separate flat shadow layer; a flat layer shows a ghost outline when the ticket tilts.
- Paper sparkle (2026-10-21, user request): the gold paper sparkles "very very very very slightly" with the light. 2,400 tiny vector glints (scripts/build-ticket.mjs) are sorted into 12 facets. Keep the sparkle faint and sparse: no stars, no glitter colors, and no glints over the inks. For performance, each facet is split into tiles and the preview draws only lit facets' tiles near the light. Shading the moving gradient over every facet across the whole ticket halved the frame rate and made screen recordings choppy.
